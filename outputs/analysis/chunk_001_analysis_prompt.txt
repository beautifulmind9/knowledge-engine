====================
KNOWLEDGE TYPES
====================

# Knowledge Types

## Principle
A broad reusable idea that explains what tends to work.

Example:
Simple ideas are easier to remember.

## Framework
A structured model, checklist, or set of parts.

Example:
Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories.

## Mental Model
A way of understanding how something works.

Example:
The curse of knowledge makes it hard to explain things clearly once you already understand them.

## Process
A sequence of actions someone can follow.

Example:
Identify the core message, remove unnecessary details, test whether the idea is memorable.

## Heuristic
A practical rule of thumb that helps with decisions.

Example:
When a message feels too complex, ask: what is the one thing people must remember?

## Observation
A useful insight or pattern noticed by the author, but not necessarily a full principle or framework.

Example:
People often assume more information makes communication better, but too much information can make an idea harder to remember.
