# Contributing to Knowledge Engine

Knowledge Engine was created by **Taneen Lewis** and is proprietary software.

## Beta contribution policy

During the current beta, unsolicited source-code or documentation contributions are not being accepted into the core product.

Feedback, bug reports, product ideas, testing observations, and use-case suggestions are welcome. Sharing feedback does not grant a license to the Knowledge Engine code or other proprietary materials, and it does not create an obligation to implement, purchase, compensate for, or exclusively use a suggestion.

Any future source-code, documentation, design, or other copyrightable contribution must be explicitly invited and covered by agreed contributor terms before it is accepted into the project.

Do not submit confidential information, API keys, private source material, or third-party copyrighted content that you do not have the right to share.

## Future community participation

Knowledge Engine may later support a broader contributor or extension ecosystem. If that happens, the applicable contributor terms, licenses, and boundaries between proprietary and openly licensed components will be published explicitly before contributions are accepted.

For licensing, partnership, or authorized contribution inquiries, contact the copyright holder.
