CI is a crisp, plain-talk statement that appears at the top of every order, specifying the plan's goal, the desired end-state of an opera- tion. At high levels of the Army, the CI may be relatively abstract: "Break the will of the enemy in the Southeast region." The CI never specifies so much detail that it risks being rendered obsolete by unpredictable events.
