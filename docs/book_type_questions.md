# Book Type Questions

## Writing / Communication
- What makes the writing clearer?
- What makes the message memorable?
- What examples show the technique?
- What can be turned into a reusable writing pattern?
- What mistakes should writers avoid?

## Strategy / Business
- What decision is this helping someone make?
- What tradeoff is being clarified?
- What operating principle is being taught?
- What examples show the principle in action?
- What warning applies to organizations?

## Psychology / Behavior
- What human behavior is being explained?
- What bias, pattern, or motivation is involved?
- What trigger or condition causes the behavior?
- How can this be applied ethically?
- What are the limits or risks?

## Finance / Investing
- What financial decision does this help with?
- What metric, signal, or rule is being explained?
- What assumptions matter?
- What risks or tradeoffs are present?
- What would make this insight invalid?

## Product / Technology
- What user problem is being solved?
- What feature, workflow, or design principle is implied?
- What user behavior does this explain?
- What decision rule can guide product choices?
- What failure mode should be avoided?

## Research / Policy
- What claim is being made?
- What evidence supports it?
- What assumptions are being used?
- What stakeholders are affected?
