# Extraction Lenses

## Universal Lens

Use for all sources.

Extract:

- Concepts
- Problems
- Insights
- Examples
- Patterns
- Warnings
- Tradeoffs
- Evidence
- Applications

---

## Writing / Communication Lens

Ask:

- What makes the message clearer?
- What makes the message memorable?
- What structure is being taught?
- What examples show the technique?
- What mistakes should be avoided?
- What can be reused as a writing pattern?

---

## Public Speaking Lens

Ask:

- How does the speaker capture attention?
- How is the speech structured?
- How does the speaker make ideas memorable?
- How are stories, pauses, rhythm, or repetition used?
- What audience problem is being solved?
- What delivery advice is given?
- What can be turned into a speaking checklist?

---

## Academic Lens

Ask:

- What claim is being made?
- What theory is being used?
- What evidence supports the claim?
- What assumptions are present?
- What definitions matter?
- What debate or gap is being addressed?
- What method or framework is used?
- What limitations are acknowledged?

---

## Fiction / Novel Lens

Ask:

- What themes are being explored?
- How are characters changing?
- What conflict drives the story?
- How does the author create emotion?
- How is dialogue used?
- How is pacing controlled?
- What voice or style patterns are present?
- What can a writer learn from this scene?

---

## Finance Lens

Ask:

- What financial decision does this help with?
- What metric, signal, or rule matters?
- What assumptions affect the conclusion?
- What risks are highlighted?
- What tradeoffs are involved?
- What examples show the principle?
- What would make the insight invalid?

## Finance Sub-Lenses

### Personal Finance
Ask:
- What behavior or habit affects money outcomes?
- What budgeting, saving, or debt rule is being taught?
- What emotional trap should be avoided?

### Investing
Ask:
- What investment principle is being taught?
- What risk-return tradeoff is discussed?
- What valuation or market signal matters?
- What investor behavior is being warned against?

### Corporate Finance
Ask:
- What business financial decision is being explained?
- What capital, cash flow, or return metric matters?
- What tradeoff is involved?

### Behavioral Finance
Ask:
- What bias or irrational behavior is being explained?
- What market mistake does it create?
- How can the investor protect against it?
